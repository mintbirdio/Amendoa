# Voice spec — Pedro's reply register (grown by rejection, v2.1 2026-09-03)

The routine prompts are generated from this file + the ⭐ exemplars in
REPLIES.md. Every Pedro correction updates this file; the git history of
this file IS the voice's evolution.

## The register (from observed posted replies)
Plain everyday words. Short sentences. An honest question or a simple
observation about the substance. Curious, direct, slightly informal
(typos survive). Prefers asking over asserting. Reply voice ≠ post voice —
posts are punchy takes; replies are conversation.

## Banned (each entry cites its origin)
- Finished AI drafts of any kind — Pedro writes every posted word (D11,
  night-0 feedback).
- Cleverness, wordplay, metaphors, punchlines (D12, night-1 feedback —
  "who writes this?").
- "it's not X, it's Y" and all negation-pivot constructions (2026 slop
  tell, RESEARCH-VOICE R-V2).
- Lists of three (same).
- Praise openers: "Great point", "This is great", "Love this" (day-0 spec).
- Hashtags, emoji (day-0 spec).
- Invented personal anecdotes, numbers, or claims about Pedro (day-0
  fabrication incident with the "I switched to tracking..." draft).
- More than ~1 sentence per seed; ~120 chars max (seeds prime, not write).

## Working hypotheses (watch, not yet rules; counts updated 2026-09-03)
- Questions to the author outperform statements — 1 support (q1 → Jason
  Fried answered), 1 counter (o3, a statement, also got Lenny's reply).
  Weaker than day 0 suggested; still weight seeds toward questions.
- Historical/numeric anchors travel well — 3 supports (o1: 3,480 imp;
  o4: 2,881; o8: 261). Best-performing cluster so far.
- Speed beats size: replies landing <15 min on 300k+ accounts get
  hundreds–thousands of imps (o1, o4, s4, o8); slower/smaller get tens.
  2 more clean cases → promote to rule (prioritize FIRST/EARLY hard).
- Short-takes (s1 type) are the old low-effort habit — allowed, not seeded.
  But note s4 (Pedro's own wordplay): 304 imp. The cleverness ban stays
  for SEEDS (D12); Pedro himself is free.
